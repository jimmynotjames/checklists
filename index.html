reChecklist

Repeatable Checklists

Learn a new routine or train a complex task until it's natural. Never forget a crucial step again.

Use also for recurring checklists, like packing lists, shopping lists, work stuff, or video game grinding.

Features

  • Free app
  • Create checklists that can be checked off and reset to be used again.
  • Can record notes for each checklist or checklist item.
  • Can reorder checklists and checklist items arbitrarily (drag and drop style).
